The urge isn't the enemy — it's a signal.
Before opening anything, pause for twenty seconds. Notice which kind of tired this is. Choose a doorway into rest.
What kind of tired is this?
bodytired
▶
Try: lie down or stretch Your body is asking for stillness or release. Three minutes flat on your back, or a slow stretch sequence. Scrolling can't deliver what muscles and nervous system are requesting.
decisiontired
▶
Try: stare out a window You've made too many micro-choices today. Don't add more by curating a feed. Look at something far away, with no decisions attached, for two minutes.
emotionaltired
▶
Try: music without tasks Something is sitting in your chest. Put on music that fits the feeling — not music to fix it. Sit with it for one song. Don't multitask. Let it move through.
socialtired
▶
Try: silence You've been "on" for too many people. Scrolling brings more people, more voices, more reactions. What you need is the opposite — no inputs, no performance, for ten minutes.
cognitivetired
▶
Try: slow repetitive activity Your brain wants to disengage from sense-making. Wash dishes. Walk a familiar route. Fold laundry. Boring, rhythmic, no input required. This is what rest feels like.
